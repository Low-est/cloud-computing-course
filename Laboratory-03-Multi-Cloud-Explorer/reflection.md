# Mission 3 Reflection

The cloud platform that impressed me the most during this activity was **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**. I was impressed by the wide range of services available and how AWS can support different types of applications and business requirements. Exploring AWS alongside Azure and Google Cloud Platform helped me understand why organizations need to evaluate cloud providers based on their specific needs rather than simply choosing the most popular option.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three platforms provide services for computing, storage, networking, databases, identity management, and Kubernetes. They also provide scalable cloud infrastructure that allows organizations to deploy applications without having to maintain all of the physical hardware themselves.

Business requirements have an important influence on cloud platform selection. Factors such as budget, existing technologies, performance requirements, scalability, security, and the type of application can determine which provider is most appropriate. For example, a company already using Microsoft technologies may benefit from Azure, while an organization focused heavily on artificial intelligence may consider GCP. The best choice depends on the specific problem the business needs to solve.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud provider requires more than knowing the names of different services. I learned to consider the client's situation and match their requirements with appropriate cloud services. The Linux investigation in KillerCoda also helped me understand how an existing server can be evaluated before migrating it to cloud virtual machine services.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has improved significantly after completing this mission. I added research about AWS, Azure, and GCP, compared their services, created recommendations for different client scenarios, and documented my Linux investigation. This activity helped me organize technical information using Markdown and gave me more confidence in making cloud platform recommendations based on business requirements.
